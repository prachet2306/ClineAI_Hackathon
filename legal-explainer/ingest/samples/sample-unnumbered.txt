This Rental Agreement is made between Mr. Rakesh Sharma, the Landlord, and Ms. Anita Das, the Tenant, for the flat at 14 Lake View Apartments, Guwahati, for a period of eleven months starting from the first day of next month.

The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 25,000 on or before the fifth day of every month by bank transfer, and the rent shall increase by 15% at the start of every year of the tenancy.

The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of Rs. 60,000 at the time of signing. The Landlord may deduct from the deposit any amounts for damages, cleaning, repainting and other charges as the Landlord deems necessary.

The Tenant shall give three months' written notice to end this Agreement. The Landlord may end this Agreement at any time by giving fifteen days' notice, and the Tenant shall vacate the flat within that period.

The Tenant shall pay for minor repairs and for the monthly society charges, and the Landlord shall bear the cost of major structural repairs to the building.

The Landlord may enter the flat at any reasonable time to inspect it, and the Tenant shall not sublet the flat or let any other person live in it without the written consent of the Landlord.

Both parties agree that any dispute under this Agreement shall be settled by talks first and, failing that, by the courts of Guwahati, and that this Agreement is governed by the laws of India.
