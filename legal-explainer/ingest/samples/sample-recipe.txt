Simple Tomato Soup

Ingredients: 4 ripe tomatoes, 1 onion, 2 cloves of garlic, 2 cups of vegetable stock, 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt and black pepper.

Method: Heat the oil in a pot and soften the chopped onion for five minutes. Add the garlic and the tomatoes and cook until they break down. Pour in the stock, simmer for twenty minutes, then blend until smooth. Season with salt and pepper and serve hot with fresh bread.
